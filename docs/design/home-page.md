# Home page design specification

Design reference for the redesigned Winzelle Projects home page (`wianvdm/winzelle-projects`, parent #90). Until cutover (#105), the page builds at `/next` behind the `VITE_HOME_NEXT` flag (`noindex`, unlinked; on in production builds since #104 so the client can check it live).

## Settled decisions

Settled constraints are fixed unless the maintainer changes them in #90.

- **Positioning:** Winzelle Projects is a family-run maintenance, repair and building company working across Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) and most of Nelson Mandela Bay depending on the job. It covers paving, walls and building, roofs, damp and leaks, cracks and structural repairs, painting and maintenance, kitchens, ceilings and carpentry, small plumbing and electrical jobs, and pools. Water leaks are one part of the work, not the headline focus.
- **Small plumbing and electrical scope:** Winzelle installs toilets, basins, shower heads and baths, and replaces plugs and light fittings. Agreed scope wording: "Small plumbing and electrical jobs: fitting toilets, basins, shower heads and baths; replacing plugs and light fittings. For work that needs a certificate of compliance, we'll point you to a registered electrician or plumber."
- **Desktop-first design:** Designed desktop-first (`1440x900` and `1280x800`), stacking cleanly to one column below `1100px` and at phone width (`390x844`).
- **Physical pinboard metaphor:** A steel-blue board (`--color-wz-field`) holding cool white sheets (`--color-wz-sheet`), warm photo prints (`--color-wz-print-paper`), and pale luggage tags (`--color-wz-tag-paper`), lit from the top left across three shadow elevations.
- **Logo:** Used unchanged from `public/content/images/winzelle-logo.jpg` on a white plate in the header and unframed in the footer. Never recoloured, cropped, rotated, redrawn or filtered.
- **Colour and accent:** Navy and steel blues matching the logo. Warm brick (`--color-wz-accent`) is reserved exclusively for WhatsApp actions so one primary action leads every screen. Facebook buttons stay navy.
- **Illustration:** Technical section and elevation drawings in a surveyor's hand, toggled between problem and fixed states, with interactive lettered callouts.
- **Contact hierarchy:** WhatsApp-first click-to-chat (`wa.me/27828781557`), phone call second (`082 878 1557`), fallback quote form below WhatsApp (kept against earlier advice to cut it). Zero paid services: form posts via Web3Forms (#101); map uses OpenStreetMap / Leaflet (#100) rather than a drawn map.
- **WhatsApp button labels:** Labels stay short and general (`WhatsApp us`, `WhatsApp us a photo`). The selected trade or photo context goes in the prefilled message, never in the button label.
- **Trade picker behaviour:** One unified "What needs doing?" picker with 10 tiles (`Kitchens, ceilings and carpentry` and `Small plumbing and electrical` included). Clicking a tile lifts it off the tray leaving its empty dashed slot ("On the sheet" / "Open below"), updates the explorer sheet, updates WhatsApp links across the page, and filters "Our work".
- **Voice and story:** First-person plural, South African spelling, speaking like the family talking to a neighbour. "Our story" stays short and secondary, placed after "Our work".
- **No invented facts:** Zero fabricated reviews, ratings, counts, years, names, guarantees or certificates. No surveyor name, years of experience, or portrait; no team photos (crews vary by contract). NHBRC is shown as registered; the registration number comes only from the `opening.nhbrcNumber` slot, which stays empty and renders nothing until the maintainer supplies the number. Photo captions name the work, suburb and month only, never a street address or client name.
- **Filler tracking:** Placeholder suburbs, dates, service-area wording and reply times were tracked as marked filler and replaced with confirmed facts in #102. Pool photography has no site photos yet and uses drawings plus a fallback message.
- **Facebook links:** Retained in "Our work" and inside the photo lightbox so visitors can inspect more jobs beyond the curated board.
- **Footer:** Full-width title block with edge-to-edge ink rules (`1px` / `1.5px`), unframed logo, labelled cells (`Address`, `Area`, `Phone`, `Email`, `Facebook`, `On this page`), non-breaking claims, `44px` to `48px` links on phones, and a single copyright row.
- **Avoid list:** No dark full-bleed grounds, no charcoal/safety-yellow palette, no portrait/team placeholders, no drawn maps, no design-only trust badges, no pill rows or eyebrow tags, no parallax, no scroll reveals, no wobbling pins, no looping animations, no chat widgets and no quote calculators.
- **CSP constraints:** `public/_headers` sets `img-src 'self' https://tile.openstreetmap.org` (map tiles, requested only after the visitor shows the map), `font-src 'self' https://fonts.gstatic.com` (Google entries kept for `/` until #105), `object-src 'none'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, `form-action 'none'`. Markup uses no inline `style=` attributes and no `data:` image URLs.

## Sections: content and behaviour

Wrapper width is `1280px` max with `48px` horizontal padding (`16px` below `1100px`), `scroll-padding-top: 72px`, and a `3px solid` `--color-wz-river` focus outline (`outline-offset: 3px`). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, transitions and animations collapse to instant updates.

### S1. Header and sticky contact bar

- **Top header (`100px` desktop height):** The page's `banner` landmark from load (`role="banner"`: it sits inside `main`, after the headline sheet in the DOM so the opening WhatsApp action stays the first Tab stop, where a `header` element maps to no landmark). Left white plate (`5px` pad) carrying the `66x66px` logo (`alt="Winzelle Projects logo: building tools forming a W, with Jos 1:9, Wees sterk en vasberade"`), company name `Winzelle Projects`, trade summary `Maintenance, repairs and building. Family-run.`, four anchor links (`What needs doing` -> `#wz-what`, `Our work` -> `#wz-work`, `Our story` -> `#wz-story`, `Contact` -> `#wz-contact`), and phone link `082 878 1557` (`tel:+27828781557`).
- **Sticky contact bar (`#wz-sticky`):** Fixed top strip inset to the board grid (`64px` desktop, `60px` below `1100px`). Hidden off-screen (`translateY(-110%)`, `visibility: hidden`) until the opening WhatsApp CTA (`#wz-hero-wa`) scrolls out of view (`bottom < 72` or `top > innerHeight`). Contains company name, section links (hidden below `1100px`), `082 878 1557` (replaced by a navy `Call` button with `aria-label="Call 082 878 1557"` below `1100px`), and `WhatsApp us`. A `region` landmark named "Contact" (`role="region"`, `aria-label="Contact"`), not a second banner: it repeats the header's links.
- **Active nav tracking:** An `IntersectionObserver` (`rootMargin: '-72px 0px -70% 0px'`) sets `aria-current="true"` on the nav link whose section occupies the band just below the sticky bar.

### S2. Opening screen

- **Headline sheet (`.wz-headline`, `600px` desktop):** Pinned sheet containing the single `h1`: "We fix, maintain and build in Port Elizabeth." Subtitle: "Paving, walls, roofs, waterproofing, painting, kitchens, and small plumbing and electrical jobs, for homes, body corporates and small businesses."
- **Primary actions:** Warm `WhatsApp us a photo` button (`#wz-hero-wa`, `60px` min-height desktop) beside `or phone 082 878 1557`, followed by "The visit and the quote are free." and the small-print note "WhatsApp is run by Meta." (S6 privacy notes).
- **NHBRC stamp (`.wz-seal`):** `112x112px` circular SVG rotated `-9deg` at the headline sheet's top-right corner (`role="img"`, `aria-label="Stamp: NHBRC registered"`), reading `registered` / `NHBRC` / `home builder`. Once supplied, the registration number (`opening.nhbrcNumber`, up to 10 digits) sits on a line under `NHBRC` inside the inner ring and joins the name (`Stamp: NHBRC registered, registration number {number}`); while the slot is empty it renders nothing.
- **Photo cluster (`.wz-cluster`):** Four tilted prints pinned right of the headline sheet (2-column grid at `1100px` to `1279px` and `<1100px`). Clicking any print opens the opening-set lightbox.

### S3. What needs doing?

Two-column layout (`minmax(380px, 452px) minmax(0, 1fr)` on desktop, the "Not sure" card under the tray and the explorer sheet beside both; single column below `1100px` where the explorer sheet sits immediately after the tray and the "Not sure" card follows it). The DOM, and so the Tab order, is tray, sheet, card at every width.

- **Trade tray (`#wz-what`):** Heading "What needs doing?", helper line ("Pick the kind of work. We'll show how it's done, our photos of it, and a WhatsApp message ready to send."), and 10 tile buttons in a 2-column grid (`role="group"`, `aria-label="Kinds of work"`). Each tile shows an `aria-hidden` fixed-state SVG crop of its trade drawing and its label. Selecting a tile sets `aria-pressed="true"`, turns the tile into a dashed empty slot (`On the sheet ->` desktop / `Open below ↓` narrow), updates the explorer sheet, updates all general WhatsApp links, filters "Our work", and scrolls the explorer panel into view if its top is above the sticky bar or its WhatsApp button sits below the viewport (always below `1100px`). A keyboard pick keeps focus on the tile, so the scroll stops where the tile would leave the window.
- **"Not sure which it is?" card:** Pinned note below the tray with heading "Not sure which it is?", text "Most jobs are two or three of these at once. Send a photo and we'll tell you what it needs.", and `WhatsApp us a photo` CTA.
- **Explorer sheet (`#wz-panel`):** Defaults to `paving` on load without marking it picked: the Paving tile shows its slot, but the general WhatsApp messages and the "Our work" filter keep their defaults until a tile is picked. Displays the trade heading, one-line summary, `WhatsApp us` button with small-caps helper note ("Opens WhatsApp with a message about {kind.noun}. Add your suburb and a photo."), and a `Show it fixed` / `Show the problem` toggle (`aria-pressed`).
- **Surveyor drawing (`600x360` viewBox):** Shared `<defs>` material filters/gradients/patterns (`concrete`, `sand`, `brick`, `granule`, `metal`, `pipe`, `downpipe`, `timber`, `shadow`, `rust`, `brick-face`, `tile-wall`, `tile-face`, arrows) plus deterministic seeded fills (`stones`, `bricks`, `pavers`, `slope`) and leader casings. Problem/fixed groups toggle via `data-state`. Interactive lettered markers (`a` to `d`) over the drawing mirror the lettered key buttons below (`ol.wz-key`, `aria-label="Parts of the drawing"`); hovering, focusing or clicking highlights the matching glow shapes and updates the caption strip; `Escape` on any key button releases the held part and clears its light (`{drawing}`, `{view}, not to scale`). Below `1220px`, in-drawing notes hide and the strip under the drawing displays the state or held-part note.
- **Explorer footer:** Left column holds heading `What the job involves` and the verbatim trade copy; right column pins a single trade photo print or the fallback note when no photo exists (`pools`, `other`).
  - General no-photo fallback note (`pools`): `No photo of a pool job of ours yet. Send us a photo of yours and we'll come and look.`
  - Catch-all no-photo fallback note (`other`): `Not on the list? Send us a photo of the job and where it is. If it's work we do, we'll come and look.`
